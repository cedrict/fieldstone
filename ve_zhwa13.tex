incompressible Maxwellian medium:
\[
{\bm \sigma} + \frac{\eta}{\mu} \frac{d {\bm \sigma}}{dt} 
=
- p {\bm 1} + \eta \frac{d {\bm\varepsilon}}{dt}
\]
where ${\bm\varepsilon}$ is the strain tensor that includes both
elastic and viscous strains and is related to displacement $\vec{u}$
as
\[
{\bm\varepsilon} = \frac12 ( \vec\nabla \vec{u} + \vec\nabla \vec{u}^T  )
\]
