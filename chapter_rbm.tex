
From Haider et al, submitted (ROM=Reduced-Order Model):
\begin{displayquote}
reduced-order models can be divided into different categories. The
difference between intrusive and non-intrusive methods is crucial. Intrusive ROMs frequently necessitate
altering the original numerical solver and require direct access to the governing equations and numerical
methods used to discretize them. Non-intrusive ROMs, on the other hand, treat the full-order model as
a black box and only use data. This makes implementation easier, especially when dealing with complex
or commercial codes.
\end{displayquote}
