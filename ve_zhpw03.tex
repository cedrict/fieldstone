\[
{\bm \sigma} =-p {\bm 1} + 2\eta \dot{\bm\varepsilon}^v
\]
\[
{\bm \sigma} =-p {\bm 1} + 2\mu {\bm\varepsilon}^e
\]
\[
\dot{\bm\varepsilon}=\dot{\bm\varepsilon}^v+\dot{\bm\varepsilon}^e
\]
Adding the first equation to the time derivatives of the second leads to
\[
{\bm \sigma} + \frac{\eta}{\mu} \dot {\bm \sigma} = 
-(p + \frac{\eta}{\mu} \dot{p}) {\bm 1} + 2 \eta  \dot{\bm\varepsilon}
\]
